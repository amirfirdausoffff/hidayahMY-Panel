Core Features

Everything You Need

Comprehensive Islamic tools designed for your daily spiritual journey.

🕐

Accurate Prayer Times

JAKIM-based prayer times for all zones in Malaysia with real-time countdown, next prayer alerts, and daily solat checklist tracker.

📖

Al-Quran with Translation

Full Quran with Uthmani script, Malay (Basmeih) and English (Sahih) translations. Includes audio player with renowned reciters.

🧭

Qiblah Compass

Precise Qiblah direction finder using your device compass. Know exactly where to face for prayer, anywhere you are.

📚

Daily Azkar & Dua

Morning and evening azkar with counter, curated dua collections for every occasion, and daily dua reminders.

🏣

Nearby Mosques

Find mosques and surau near you with GPS-based search, distance indicators, and navigation via Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze.

📌

Hajj & Umrah Guide

Step-by-step interactive guides for both Hajj and Umrah rituals with detailed explanations and visual aids.
